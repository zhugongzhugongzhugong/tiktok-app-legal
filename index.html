Zhu Video Publisher

Publish your finished videos to TikTok in one step.

Zhu Video Publisher is a personal publishing tool for a creator who makes short technical videos about industrial automation. It uploads each finished video to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, so the same video doesn't have to be uploaded by hand on every platform.

Open the publisher

What it does

Log in with TikTok

Connect your account with TikTok Login Kit. The app shows which account it will post to before anything is uploaded.

Post directly

Choose the caption, who can view the video, and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, then post to your profile.

Or send as a draft

Send the video to your TikTok inbox instead, and finish editing and posting it in the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. Open the publisher and select Continue with TikTok. TikTok asks you to log in and approve access.
  2. Pick a video file and preview it.
  3. Choose Post directly (set caption, privacy and interaction settings) or Send as a draft.
  4. The app uploads the video to TikTok and shows when it's done.
  5. Select Disconnect at any time to revoke access.

Your data

The app only uses your basic TikTok profile (display name, username and avatar) to show which account is connected, and an access token to upload the videos you choose. It does not read your other videos, followers or messages, and never posts anything you didn't select. See the Privacy Policy for details.

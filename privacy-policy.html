Privacy Policy

Zhu Video Publisher · Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. What this app does

Zhu Video Publisher is a personal tool its owner uses to upload videos and publish them to their own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

2. Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account, and only within the permissions you approve, the app receives:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your open_id, display name, username and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Posting settings returned by TikTok: available privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are turned off for your account, and the maximum video length, used to show you valid posting options.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos (video.upload) and publish posts (video.publish) to your account.
  • The video files you select, together with the caption and settings you choose.

The app does not access your other videos, followers, likes, comments or messages.

3. How we use it

This information is used only to upload and publish the videos you choose, with the settings you choose. The app never posts anything without your action. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Sharing

Videos, captions and settings are sent only to TikTok, to complete the post you requested. We do not sell, rent or share your information with any other party.

5. Storage and retention

  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the local publishing service. They are not stored on this website or on any third-party server.
  • Video files are passed to TikTok during upload and are not kept by the app afterward.
  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect. Selecting Disconnect in the publisher revokes the authorization with TikTok and deletes the stored tokens.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect from the publisher at any time, or remove the app's access from your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). After that the app can no longer access your account.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Privacy Policy

Zhu Video Publisher · Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. What this app does

Zhu Video Publisher is a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos and publish them to their own TikTok account via TikTok's official API. It is not offered to the public.

2. Data we access

When you authorize the app through TikTok's OAuth flow, the app receives:

  • Basic TikTok account identifiers (open_id, display name) to identify the connected account;
  • An access token that permits the app to upload videos and publish posts to your TikTok profile, strictly within the permissions you grant.

3. How data is used

Your data is used solely to operate the app's core function: publishing videos you choose to your own TikTok account. The app does not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

4. Data storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as needed to maintain the TikTok connection. Video files you choose to publish are transmitted to TikTok and are not retained longer than necessary for the upload.

5. Your rights

You may revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings. Upon revocation the app can no longer access your account, and stored tokens are deleted.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the TikTok developer portal.
